<p>Trends come and go. Cottagecore, dark academia, japandi, coastal grandmother - every year a new aesthetic takes over Pinterest and Instagram for six months and then quietly fades.</p>
<p><span class="important">Van Gogh has been popular for over a century. That is not a trend. That is something else.</span></p>
<p>Part of it is the colour - those impossible blues, the vibrating yellows, the way his palettes feel both restless and warm at the same time. Part of it is simply that the work is good in a way that does not require explanation.</p>
<p>For people who love art, decor, and the feeling of a room that looks intentional rather than assembled - Van Gogh-inspired pieces are a natural anchor. They work in modern apartments.
