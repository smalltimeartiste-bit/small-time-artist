<p>For people who have moved away, Kolkata-themed <a href="/products/the-gogh-edit/the-wall-edit">wall art</a> does something quietly powerful. <p>Whether it is a hand-painted cityscape, a Bengali quote rendered in art, or an abstract piece built around colours and textures that feel distinctly Kolkata - art has a way of making unfamiliar rooms feel inhabited. Lived in. Yours.</p>
<p><em>Bengali wall art and Kolkata-themed home decor have seen a quiet surge in demand among people who have relocated - because surrounding yourself with something familiar is one of the gentlest ways to take care of yourself in an unfamiliar place.</em></p>
